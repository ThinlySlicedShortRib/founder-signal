A Podcasts tab in the Library

Every episode you've made or played, in one place. It's the Library's third tab, beside Saved and Letters, and it uses the same page: the big title, the black switch, the same tints. Episodes sit in a compact list grouped by where you are with them, and each row shows its state at a glance: ready to play, playing, part-way, listened, being made, or failed. Tap play and the episode starts right there. The bottom pill grows the same status row as the approved background-task design, so it keeps playing while you browse. Tap the row's text to open the piece at its Listen card.

A list, not cards

Audio is picked by state, not by cover. A row is about 70px, so six or seven episodes fit on a screen, where the Saved tiles fit three. The play button sits in the same spot on every row, under the thumb. The piece's tint moves into the button (for listened and being made) and into the tinted highlight on the playing row, so it still looks like the Library. Switch to Tinted cards above to compare: they read well but you scroll a lot further to reach older episodes.

Grouped by where you are with it

In progress comes first, because carrying on is the most likely tap and it's rarely more than a row or two. New is next, with episodes being made and any that failed, since those are fresh requests. Listened is last and dimmed. Each group is newest first.

Not by letter: episodes are made one piece at a time, so most letters would hold a single row. Each row names its letter instead (“No. 14 · Piece 2”). Rows don't jump between groups while you're on the tab. They regroup the next time you open it.

A row's states

  • Ready: a blue play button, with the length.
  • Playing: pause, moving bars, time left and a small progress line, on a light blue row.
  • Part-way: play, “2:10 left” and the progress line.
  • Listened: a check, dimmed. Tap to play it again.
  • Being made: the approved progress ring with its step and time left (“~40s left”), and ✕ to stop. Stop asks first, in the row, and counts as Keep after five seconds. When the ring closes it becomes the play button.
  • Failed: an orange mark and Retry.

Playing from the tab

Play starts the episode in place. The pill grows the approved status row: pause, the chapter, the time, and the scrubber. It stays while you switch tabs or open a piece, and it folds when the episode ends. Tap the row's text, or an episode's title in the list, to open that piece at its Listen card.

On this tab, an episode being made already shows in its own row, so the pill's row is kept for playback. Everywhere else, the approved rule stands.

Empty

Before there are any episodes, a short note says how to make one, with a small headphones drawing and the cover's own headphones button to look for.

Small extras Optional

  • Play all new: plays the New episodes one after another. While it runs it reads “Playing new · 1 of 2”. Tap it again to stop after the current one.
  • Remove: swipe a row left or press and hold, then Remove, with Undo for a few seconds. It only leaves your list: episodes are shared per piece, so nothing is deleted.
  • Downloaded: a small arrow beside the length on episodes kept for offline listening, plus a note at the end of the list, like Saved's.
