Founder Signal · Listen · play and make (design mock)

Listening to one episode while another is made

Today the pill's row shows one episode at a time, whichever was started last. Start episode B while A is playing and the row jumps to B's progress, or flips between the two, and A's pause button and scrubber disappear. Each design below keeps A's controls where they are and shows B somewhere else. All four run through the same scenario, on a piece page and in Library, with both Listen cards and the Podcasts tab beside them.

Recommendation · A, playback owns the row

The row is always the episode you can hear, on every screen, so nothing you're using moves. B shows up as a small ring with its time left at the row's right end, then as a Ready chip that asks Play now or Play after this. It's the smallest change to the approved row, the choice stays with the reader, and the chip grows into a count and a list when more than one episode is being made.

A is “Why usage-based pricing keeps winning at the seed stage” (piece 1). B is “The board meeting you actually want to run” (piece 2). Making B is sped up from about a minute to about ten seconds. In each design, the left phone follows the reader across the pages, and the right phone shows the same moment from Library's Podcasts tab.
