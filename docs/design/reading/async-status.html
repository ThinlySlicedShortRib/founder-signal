Where background work shows its status

Making a Listen episode takes about a minute, so it runs in the background. One small strip shows how it's going, and it lives in the same place in both modes. It sits in the bottom pill as a row above the buttons, on the cover and in the article alike. Opening or closing the article swaps the buttons under it, and the strip stays where it is. While the episode is being made, a small ring fills clockwise with the real steps: writing the script takes the first quarter, recording the voices fills most of the rest one line at a time, and finishing closes it. The step and an estimate of the time left sit beside it (“Recording voices · ~40s left”), counting down steadily. When the ring closes, it turns into the blue play button. The Listen card under the byline is still where a reader starts an episode, and it shows the same ring at the same point. Both phones share the same task, so the buttons below move them together. Tapping ✕ while an episode is being made asks first: the row turns into “Stop this episode?” with Keep and Stop, and it goes back to making on its own after five seconds, or when you tap elsewhere or scroll.
