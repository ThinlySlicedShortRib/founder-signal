Founder Signal · Listen · expandable player (design mock)

One row for what you hear, one drag for the rest

Design A kept the row on the episode you can hear, but squeezed a chip in beside the scrubber. Here the row goes back to full width and only ever shows what's playing. A handle on its top edge says more is waiting. Drag the row up, or tap the handle, and it grows into a dark panel out of the same pill, with the pill's controls still underneath: Now playing, then Up next, Ready and Making, ordered by how soon you'll hear them.

Hint · the handle fills

The handle you drag is the hint. While an episode is being made it fills left to right with the progress of the one closest to done, and when one is ready it turns blue and pulses once. It adds nothing to the row and still reads as “something is moving under here”. The alternative, a small count dot beside the handle, is easier to spot and says how many, but it's one more badge in a row we just cleared, and a grey “1” doesn't say whether it's nearly done. Switch between them in the bar below.

Hint

A is “Why usage-based pricing keeps winning at the seed stage” (piece 1). B is “The board meeting you actually want to run” (piece 2). Making B is sped up from about a minute to about ten seconds. Drag either row up or down, tap its handle, or use the toggle under each phone. The dashed speed button is optional.

Why it's calmer. Collapsed, the row has one job again: the episode you can hear, at full width, with nothing beside the scrubber. Everything else waits one drag away, and the only trace of it is the handle filling.

With 3 or more. The collapsed row doesn't change at all; the handle just follows whichever episode is closest to done. The panel gets longer and scrolls inside, capped at about half the screen, so more episodes never squeeze the player.

What's in the panel. Now playing, larger: title, letter and piece, a big scrubber with chapter marks and times, ±15s and play or pause. Then Up next (episodes you chose to play after this, each with ✕), Ready (Play now or Play after this), Making (ring, step, time left, and ✕ with the approved Stop confirm), and a link to all episodes in Podcasts.

The hint, up close

The same collapsed row in three moments, with each hint style. A is playing in every one; only the handle changes.

Handle fills Recommended

Nothing waiting: a plain grey handle. Making: it grows a little and fills white with the progress. Ready: it turns blue and pulses once, then stays blue until you open the panel or the episode plays.

Count dot Alternative

A small number beside the handle: grey while episodes are being made, blue once one is ready, popping once when it changes. Clearer at a glance, but busier, and it can't show how close anything is.

Four waiting, and nothing playing

This phone is clickable. Play now, Play after this, ✕ on Up next, ✕ on Making (with the Stop confirm), play or pause, ±15s and speed all work, and the two episodes being made finish in about half a minute.

Four waiting. The collapsed row is the same as with one. Open, the panel scrolls: one Up next, one Ready and two Making, with Now playing pinned above them. When A ends, the Up next episode plays, because you asked for it; Ready ones never start on their own.

Nothing playing, one making. The row shows the episode being made, as approved: ring, step, time left and ✕. The handle stays plain because the only thing waiting is already in the row. When it's ready, the row becomes its play button, and the panel opens the same way.
