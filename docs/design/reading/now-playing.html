One episode, carried across the app

An episode keeps playing when you leave its piece. The bottom pill carries it everywhere as the approved status row: play and pause, the chapter, the time and a scrubber. The row sits above whatever the screen puts in the pill (the pager's arrows, the screen's name and menu, the article's reading controls). When you change screens, only those controls morph. The row stays where it is, at least 264px wide, and keeps counting. Tap its text from anywhere to go back to the piece, with the article open at its Listen card. When the episode ends, the row says so and folds away. If another one starts, the row swaps to it in place.

The row stays put

The row is claimed apart from the controls (PillStatusSlot), so a new screen only morphs what's under it: the old controls fade and shrink a touch, the new ones fade up in turn. The row doesn't fold, grow or fade, and the time keeps counting. For that to hold across Library, Activity and Preferences, the pill's provider moves up to the app layout, so every screen claims the same pill.

On the narrow pill

Library, Activity and Preferences carry only their name and the menu button, about 148px. With the row the pill is 264px, the row's least width, and the controls sit centred under it with a hairline between. The pager (240px) and the menu-open pills widen to 264px the same way, so moving between them the pill doesn't change width at all. Only the article's reading controls are wider (268px), and the row takes their width.

With the menu open Row stays

The row stays above the menu-open pill, and the sheet slides up under both. The pill already sits over the menu sheet, so you can pause while you choose where to go. Hiding the row would mean folding it and growing it again every time the menu opens, which is the motion this design avoids. The sheet's bottom padding already counts the row, so its last item clears it.

Back to the piece

Tap the row's text from any screen. The app opens the episode's letter at that piece, then the article rises with the Listen card in view, and the card flashes once. The play button and the scrubber only act in place; they never move you.

When it ends, or another starts

  • End: the row reads “Listened · 4 min” with its check, holds for a moment, then folds away, wherever you are.
  • Another episode: start one from the Podcasts tab and the row stays open. Its text and progress crossfade to the new episode, with no fold and no regrow.
  • Up next Optional with “Play all new” running, the row shows “Up next” and the title for a moment, then moves on.

Playback doesn't stop

The episode keeps playing when the phone locks, when you switch to another app, and when the app is in the background. Only you, the end of the episode, or another audio app stops it.

The now playing card

  • Title: the piece's title, cut to one line.
  • Under it: “Founder Signal · No. 14 · Piece 2”, so it's clear where it came from.
  • Artwork: the piece's own art, the same picture as its page.
  • Controls: play and pause, back and forward 15 seconds, and a scrubber. Headphone buttons and Control Center do the same.

It's one player, so the card and the row always agree: pause on the lock screen and the row shows play when you come back. Try it here; both follow the same episode.

When it ends

The card goes when the episode ends, at the same moment the row folds. If another episode starts, the card swaps to its title and art.
